TORRANCE, Calif. – April 8, 2025 – GITAI USA Inc. (GITAI), a U.S.-headquartered space robotics company, today announced the establishment of its new subsidiary, GITAI Defense and Space LLC, incorporated in Delaware and operating from the company’s headquarters in Torrance, California.
