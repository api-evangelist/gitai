TORRANCE, Calif. – December 23, 2025 – GITAI USA Inc. (GITAI), a U.S.-headquartered space robotics company, today announced that it has been awarded a contract under the Missile Defense Agency’s (MDA) Scalable Homeland Innovative Enterprise Layered Defense (SHIELD) Indefinite-Delivery/Indefinite-Quantity (IDIQ) program.
